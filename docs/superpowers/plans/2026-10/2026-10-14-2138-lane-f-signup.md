## Overrides
1. Account (step 1) and agency (step 2) are collected client-side; **`POST /signup` fires at the end of step 2** (spec decision 1). `email_taken` returns the user to step 1 with the message; `weak_password` inline. Passwords are never written to storage (`grep -rniE 'localStorage|sessionStorage' app/javascript/features/signup` prints nothing).
2. Qualified definition uses `QualifiedRadioCards` (recommended pre-selected: Attended a consult) and time zone uses `TimezoneChips`; both from the kit.
3. Step 3 (ad accounts) is optional: Meta/Google rows with 38px logo tiles (`ChannelTile`-style tiles), Connect → green "Connected" pill-button; copy states spend is **sample** until live sync ("Sample spend — live sync coming").
4. **Step 4 is where owners load their real data** (the seven exports; Lakeside is 69 MB / ~625k rows, so everything after the upload request is **asynchronous**). It composes the kit pieces with `useSnapshotUpload`: `Dropzone` → `upload(files)` (`state: uploading`) → `validating` with `UploadProgress` (`Reading your files` → `Checking your data`) → `review` with `ReviewReport` ("Load data" disabled when `report.ok === false`; errors listed per file/row) → "Load data" (`commit()`; single-flight) → `committing` with `UploadProgress` (`Importing rows (24,000 of 52,000)` → `Building your insights`) → `done` ("Your data is loaded"). The user may press **Continue** to step 5 at any time while a load runs (it continues in the background; leaving or reloading resumes via `useCurrentSnapshot().pending`); **Open my dashboard** is always enabled and the dashboard shows `LoadingYourData` until the load finishes. `FileChecklist` shows Loaded/Waiting as files are matched; "Skip for now" allowed; `409 already_in_progress` → "A load is already running"; the shield note "Screener answers are encrypted" is always visible. Several agencies may sign up with the same files; nothing in the UI mentions other agencies.
5. Step 5: invite with email + coordinator picker (options from `useCoordinatorNames()`; free-text not allowed); list shows the owner (brand pill) and invites (amber "Invited" pill) with a **Copy invite link** button; unknown names show `valid_names` from `ApiError.extra`. Final CTA "Open my dashboard" → `/`.
6. The wizard is a public route outside the app shell: centered card (max 600px, `rounded-modal`), mark 40px above the stepper; mobile: card fills the width, footer bar sticky at the bottom.

## Responsive acceptance
- **Desktop:** header strip with five numbered dots (current `bg-brand`, done `bg-success` ✓, upcoming white + border) and labels; body `28px 24px`; footer Back/Continue.
- **Mobile (390×844):** labels collapse to "Step N of 5" under the dots; dropzone and review rows stack; Continue stays reachable above the keyboard (sticky footer); no horizontal scroll.
