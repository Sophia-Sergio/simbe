## Overrides
1. `AgentProvider` supplies `{ isOpen, open(question?), close() }` through the Wave 0 `AgentContext`; `open(question)` opens the panel and auto-sends the question once (e.g. the "Ask why" buttons on This week and the channel detail "Ask Simbe" button).
2. The FAB is hidden while `useLeadPanel().leadId` is set and when the panel is open; on mobile the **Ask tab** (nav item above) opens the full-screen panel — no FAB.
3. Message rendering is escape-only (no HTML); `action` button navigates with `useNavigate` and closes the panel; `sources` chips navigate; `suggestions` chips send; pending shows a pulsing "Reading your data…"; composer sends with Enter, disabled while pending; conversation id kept in component state (no storage).
4. Errors: `409` → "Still working on your last question."; `502` → "I couldn't reach the model. Try again." (keeps the typed text); `422 text_too_long` → inline counter at 500 chars.
5. Mock answers: default `contracts/examples/agent/answer.json` (chart + action + sources + follow-ups); `?mock=error` → 502; `?mock=slow` → delayed (for the pending/409 tests).
6. Owner chips (4) and coordinator chips (3) come from `getAgentSuggestions`.
